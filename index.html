Coaching

Everything a coaching client needs, in one shared space.

Plans that adapt

Start each cycle from a program template, then tailor it as you go. Goals, sessions, reading and challenges stay in order.

Sessions with a record

Keep private notes, share a summary and the takeaways, and attach recordings to the people who were there.

Tasks, wins and check-ins

You and your client both add tasks. Wins, journals and worksheets build a picture of progress you can both see.

A library you can assign

Lessons, exercises and resources, linked by the ideas you teach. Send one to a person or a whole group.

Private when it matters

Clients can mark any entry private. Coach-only details stay with the coach. Nothing is deleted by accident.

Clients to contracts

One record carries someone from lead to client to alumnus, with the offerings and contracts that start each engagement.

Teams

Run your team on a steady weekly rhythm.

Seats and responsibilities, a weekly scorecard, quarterly rocks, issues and to-dos, and a simple way to run and rate your weekly meeting, shared by everyone on the team.

  • Accountability chart with who owns each seat
  • Scorecard numbers entered by the team each week
  • Rocks, issues and to-dos in one list
  • Weekly meetings with an agenda and a 1–10 rating

  4. Watch the progress. Tasks, wins and ratings show you what's working.
