The 90-day cycle

Long enough to change. Short enough to stay focused.

Ninety days is a quarter: enough time to build real habits and see real results, short enough that the finish line stays in sight. When one cycle ends, the next one starts from where you got to.

  1. Days 1–10

    Baseline

    Take stock with your coach: where you are in life, leadership and fitness, and the one goal in each that would change the most.

  2. Days 11–80

    Build

    Regular sessions, weekly tasks and daily habits. Lessons and exercises arrive when you need them, and every win goes on the record.

  3. Days 81–90

    Review and reset

    Look back at what moved, celebrate it, and set up the next 90 days to build on it.
